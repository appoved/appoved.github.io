Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications developed and published by Yury Bondar (Appoved), including FlyMoney and other utility applications (collectively, the "Apps").

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The Apps are designed to work without user registration or accounts. Preferences and operational data — such as selected currencies, active input fields, and cached exchange rates for offline use — are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our personal servers. We do not request or collect precise GPS location data.

2. Third-Party Services and Data Collection

To provide up-to-date functionality and keep the Apps free, we integrate reliable third-party services:

  • Advertising (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK): Our Apps display banner advertisements via Yandex Mobile Ads. This SDK may automatically collect technical device information, IP address, and advertising identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or Google Advertising ID on Android) to serve, measure, and personalize ads. You can learn more in the Yandex Privacy Policy.
  • Exchange Rate Providers (Frankfurter API): To fetch reference currency rates, the Apps send standard HTTP requests to public financial APIs (such as Frankfurter). These requests transmit only technical network headers (like IP address) necessary to deliver the data and contain no personal user information.

3. Anonymous Product Analytics

We may collect aggregated, anonymized technical telemetry (such as app version, operating system, device type, crash diagnostics, and general interface interactions) to improve stability and usability. Financial amounts entered by users are never logged or transmitted.

4. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your region (including the EEA/GDPR and California/CCPA), you have the right to control personalized advertising and local data:

  • You can reset or limit the use of your advertising identifier in your device's privacy settings (iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or Android Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The Apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
